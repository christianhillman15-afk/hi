Our story

A builder's reputation is only as good as its last job site.

That's the standard DK3 Construction was built on. We started as a hands‑on crew that believed the details others rush — the framing you never see, the grade beneath the slab, the caulk line at the finish — are exactly what separate a good build from a great one.

Today we're a full‑service general contractor delivering luxury residential and commercial projects across Fort Lauderdale and greater South Florida. We've earned the trust of homeowners and hospitality groups alike by doing what we say, on the schedule we promised, at the quality we're known for. From site excavation to the final walkthrough, one accountable team stands behind the work.
